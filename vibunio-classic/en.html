Vibunio Classic — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Who operates Vibunio

The controller is Yari Andrea Cassetta, a natural person established in Italy. Vibunio Classic is the standard version of the Vibunio app; YK Labs Studio is the controller's brand, not an incorporated company or a separate legal entity. The privacy and support contact is yklabs.studio@gmail.com.

You can contact the controller directly at this email address. No professional address is available; no home address is published.

2. Gameplay and data on your device

You can play without an account or connection. The Italian/English cards are bundled with the app; no online service generates or translates them. The app has no gameplay backend.

The app stores the following in its local preferences:

• optional participant names;

• language and vibration preferences;

• game mode, mood and session state, the current card, A/B selection, turn and counters;

• previously displayed card IDs and the last card type, to avoid repeats;

• only with the advertising integration enabled, a random session identifier for ad controls;

• in builds with advertising, the timestamp and session ID of an interstitial actually shown, to limit ad frequency.

The release without advertising neither generates nor saves this random identifier. At startup, its field is removed from any readable previous saves while preserving the other gameplay data.

Application code does not send these names or gameplay records to YK Labs or advertising providers. It does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone or GPS location. It does not use a device identifier to recognise participants.

These records support gameplay, restoration, preferences and limits on repeats or ad frequency. No automatic time-based expiry is implemented. Session state is updated during gameplay; once every card in a compatible pool has been displayed, that pool's IDs are removed from anti-repeat history to allow a new cycle. You can remove local records by clearing Vibunio's app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app. The app's backup configuration excludes preferences, cloud backup and application-data transfer. Local deletion does not delete emails or data already processed by providers.

2.1. Age in the release without advertising

The release without advertising does not ask for or retain an age range. Starting the app removes any previously stored age preference without deleting sessions, names, language or gameplay history. UMP and Google Mobile Ads are not started.

Test builds with test ads retain a separate optional on-device choice: this does not represent active collection in the distributed release. In those builds the range is not added to emails or sent to a controller-operated server. Bringing collection back to a release requires a new assessment before advertising activation.

3. Email feedback

“Report a problem” and “Suggest an improvement” open an editable draft in your email client. The app never sends a message automatically.

The draft contains the text you choose to write and these technical details: app version, Android version, device model, app language and device language. It does not add your age range, participant names, history, GPS coordinates, logs or attachments. You can edit or remove its contents before sending.

If you choose to send the email, the client transmits it to the email service. The recipient may receive the message, your email address and transmission information handled by the service; delivery depends on email services and is not guaranteed by Vibunio. Email clients and providers process the message under their own terms. The intended purpose is to respond to feedback and provide support.

The controller manages the mailbox personally using free consumer Gmail, not Google Workspace. Support emails are ordinarily deleted within 6 months after the request is closed. The controller reviews them periodically and manually deletes relevant messages, folders and copies under their control. The app does not perform this deletion.

Longer retention concerns only data needed for an identified legal obligation or a specific dispute. The controller must record the reason and the period or event ending the need, review them periodically and delete data when no longer needed. Indefinite archiving of the entire mailbox is not envisaged.

For privacy requests, data is processed until the request and any remaining necessary steps are completed. Attachments, identification documents and copies of requested data are removed as soon as they are no longer needed. At closure, only minimal evidence still needed for a documented purpose is retained, recording the reason, necessary data and final period or event. The controller conducts a monthly manual review and deletes anything no longer necessary, including relevant copies under their control. No automatic 36-month period applies. An identified legal obligation or specific dispute may justify retaining only the necessary subset until that need ends. Provider retention remains separate from the mailbox's retention.

4. Google AdMob and provider data

Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.5.0 and Google UMP 4.0.0 are present. The first release keeps live advertising disabled: release application code does not start UMP or Google Mobile Ads and does not request ads. Any future activation requires a new Play/AdMob assessment and would permit only non-personalised ads for users declaring they are 18 or older, subject to applicable privacy controls. Debug builds use only Google's official test ad IDs, with the same eligibility controls. No future activation or date is promised. Ads are not required to play offline.

Only with an 18+ range, an enabled integration, suitable connectivity and ad requests permitted by UMP, the ads SDK can collect/share IP addresses, interactions, diagnostics and available device/account identifiers, including advertising and app-set IDs. An IP address can indicate approximate location. Google describes advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes, with TLS-encrypted transmission. These are provider operations: “offline-first” and “non-personalised ads” do not mean no data collection or privacy obligations. Ad personalisation is explicitly disabled in the SDK configuration before initialisation.

5. Consent, UMP and identifiers

The current release disables the advertising path for everyone: no age range or advertising consent is required to play or read this policy. The following behaviour concerns test builds with test ads and would require a new assessment for a future release with advertising. In the verified paths for ranges under 18 or unknown, application code does not start UMP or Google Mobile Ads and does not request ads. This includes users who do not answer or choose “Prefer not to say”. Only after an 18+ choice, with a configured integration and suitable connectivity, does application code access UMP and request a consent-information update over the network. It may process technical app/device information, regional signals and privacy choices needed to present Google's messages. The SDKs manage their own preferences and consent information on the device; cross-app consent synchronisation is not configured.

Application code does not request ads when UMP does not permit them. Eligibility to request ads does not authorise personalisation: only non-personalised ads are contemplated for any future activation. Applicable privacy controls and choices remain necessary; UMP does not verify the user's age. If needed, UMP presents its form on Home. In Settings, “Privacy options” lets you review or change choices, including withdrawal where offered by the form, when UMP requires that entry point. Changing consent does not automatically delete data already processed by providers. Network errors do not prevent gameplay. Reading this policy and playing do not depend on an age-range choice or advertising consent.

Changing the range immediately blocks newly disallowed requests and invalidates preloaded ads; banners managed by the app are destroyed. The change does not undo requests already started or earlier processing while the adult path was eligible, nor delete data already transmitted. These limitations are not a guarantee that the operating system, Google or other providers perform no processing whatsoever.

The app does not set its own tracking cookies. Provider SDKs or forms may use local storage and identifiers according to their configuration. You can also manage the advertising ID in Android settings. UMP is not described as an automatic guarantee of compliance.

Reference: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy

6. Legal grounds and transfers

The grounds below concern different purposes. We do not request general consent to play or send a request.

• Local gameplay and preferences: the purpose is to provide the requested offline game and restore sessions and preferences, based on the controller's legitimate interest, GDPR Article 6(1)(f). Processing is limited to section 2's features: optional names, no profiling or application-code transmission, and deletion through Android. The controller defines these purposes but does not receive local records. No age range is retained in the release without ads.

• Voluntary support: the controller processes the sender's address and relevant content to respond to the request, based on the legitimate interest in handling support, GDPR Article 6(1)(f). Marketing, profiling and further feedback uses are not envisaged. Support is limited to the data needed to respond, with section 3's retention and the right to object. Do not send sensitive data or documents unnecessary for the request.

• Exercise of rights: insofar as necessary to meet applicable GDPR response duties, the ground is Article 6(1)(c), in conjunction with Articles 12–22. Evidence of handling does not authorise retention of all attachments; section 3's criteria apply. For a specific dispute, Article 6(1)(f) is assessed separately; a retention obligation requires an identified legal rule.

• Google: the controller processes received messages for support or privacy requests. Google operates consumer Gmail under its own terms and privacy policy, for the purposes and grounds described there. Google states that it does not act as a processor for consumer Gmail and offers no data processing agreement for it. No nonexistent Workspace contract or Article 28 agreement is asserted. The controller uses this mailbox to read and respond personally; Google is not described as a contractual processor where the consumer service provides no such arrangement. Choosing this service does not remove the controller's duties for received messages or Google's processing described in its notices.

• Advertising: the SDKs are present but the advertising path is disabled in the current release. Before future activation, grounds and recipients must be defined for each Google/UMP purpose, applicable device-storage access rules followed and any required consents obtained. Non-personalised ads for adults do not mean no processing or exemption from required consent. These requirements are not represented as already met.

Distributing the app in other countries does not itself mean transferring personal data outside the European Economic Area. Local gameplay creates no flow to a controller-operated server. Sending emails, any enabled SDK requests and opening web pages instead involve their respective services.

Google documents servers in different countries and processing outside the user's country. It describes adequacy decisions, Google LLC's Data Privacy Framework certification and standard contractual clauses where applicable. This describes provider mechanisms, not evidence of clauses executed by the controller or coverage of every Vibunio flow. Consumer Gmail is subject to Google's terms and notices applicable to the service and the account's country version. Google's stated safeguards operate within their scope; they are not automatically extended to every email recipient or third-party service. Future advertising requires a separate assessment before activation. Safeguards and related contacts are available on Google's transfer-frameworks page. No absence of international transfers is asserted.

8. Rights and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability, as well as withdrawal of consent and complaints to the competent authority. Each right depends on the processing and law; withdrawal does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful.

Contact yklabs.studio@gmail.com with a Vibunio-related request. Gameplay records stay on your device and can be deleted directly in Android. For provider-held data, also use their privacy tools. For complaints in Italy, you can contact the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali: https://www.garanteprivacy.it/it/i-miei-diritti . The GDPR also permits complaints to the supervisory authority in the Member State of habitual residence, place of work or alleged infringement. Article 12 requires a response without undue delay and normally within one month; an extension, up to two further months in the cases provided for, must be explained and notified within the first month. Additional identity-verification information may be requested where reasonable doubts exist, only as necessary and proportionate; additional data need not be collected solely to identify local records the controller cannot link to you. The controller personally handles requests received by email, determines which data is actually available to them and applies the necessary deadlines and checks described here. Reviews and deletion are manual; the evidence criterion is in section 3 and has been approved by the controller. Do not send identity documents unless their need has been explained and they have been requested.

If processing relies on legitimate interests, the right to object follows Article 21(1); for direct marketing, objection requires that use to stop under Article 21(2)–(3). Article 17 permits limits to erasure only in applicable cases, including legal obligations and the need to establish, exercise or defend legal claims; it is not general permission to retain everything.

Requirements for minors and regional rules depend on the distribution countries.
