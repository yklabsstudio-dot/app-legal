Shopping List + Loyalty Cards

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. App developer

Shopping List + Loyalty Cards is developed by YK Labs Studio. For privacy questions, contact yklabs.studio@gmail.com .

2. Shopping lists and loyalty cards

Shopping lists, products, history and loyalty card data are stored locally on the device using an Android database.

No account is required. YK Labs Studio does not operate a remote server receiving these contents. Shopping list contents and loyalty card codes are not provided as advertising SDK request parameters.

The database is excluded from the automatic backup configured by the application.

3. Camera permission

The app may request camera permission to scan loyalty card barcodes and QR codes.

You may deny or revoke camera permission in Android settings and continue entering codes manually.

4. Advertising and Google services

The release configuration currently prepared does not include Google's advertising SDKs. A future ad-supported version using Google AdMob is planned.

Once enabled, Google Mobile Ads may process information including:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • Advertising and application interactions.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.
  • Device and advertising identifiers, where available and permitted.

Google may use such information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. This section will be reviewed against the actual release configuration before advertising is activated.

More information: Google Privacy Policy .

5. Advertising consent

In versions that include advertising, the app will use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect and manage advertising preferences where required.

Privacy choices will be available through the applicable in-app options. Refusing consent will not prevent access to the app's core shopping list and loyalty card features.

6. Data retention and deletion

You can delete lists, products and loyalty cards using the app's available features. You may also clear local application data through Android settings.

Where Google services are enabled, information processed by Google is subject to Google's applicable data retention and deletion policies.

7. Security

Shopping list and loyalty card contents are stored within the Android application's local storage. Advertising services, when enabled, use their own security measures for network communications.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, external services or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published at this address.
